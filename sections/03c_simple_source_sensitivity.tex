\subsection{Background of Rotating Monopole Analysis} \label{subsec:BG_rotating}

The second test approaches the second characteristic of the rotor noise by modelling a sound source in subsonic motion. Following the work by Ffowcs Williams \cite{crighton1992modern}, a single monopole point rotates at specific Mach numbers while emitting pressure waves. The radiation frequency ($f_{source}$) is defined as an integer multiple ($n$) of the rotational frequency ($f_{rot}$), such that $f_{source}=nf_{rot}$.
